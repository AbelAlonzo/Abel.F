Hair Types

Straight Hair

Straight hair requires specific care to maintain its shine and natural softness.

  • Use hydrating shampoo
  • Apply conditioner regularly
  • Avoid excessive heat
  • Protect from sun

Wavy Hair

Natural waves need products that define and maintain shape without frizzing.

  • Use wave products
  • Dry with diffuser
  • Apply styling cream
  • Avoid brushing when dry

Curly Hair

Curls need intense hydration and products that define them without weighing them down.

  • Deep hydration
  • Use natural oils
  • Comb with fingers
  • Avoid sulfates
